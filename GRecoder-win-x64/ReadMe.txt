About GRecorder v1.0.0:
-----------------------
GRecorder is a professional, high-performance screen recording application
developed by GeoTechnology. Designed for Windows, it provides a seamless,
lag-free recording experience for content creators, gamers, educators, and
professionals.
Whether you are recording a 4K gameplay session, creating a detailed tutorial,
or capturing a quick presentation, GRecorder delivers crisp video and
crystal-clear audio with minimal impact on your system's performance.
-------------
Key Features:
-------------
✅ High-Quality Video Recording (Up to 60 FPS)
✅ Advanced Audio Mixing (System + Microphone)
✅ Intelligent Floating Panel (Collapse to edge of screen)
✅ Global Hotkeys (F9: Start/Stop, F10: Pause, F11: Screenshot)
✅ Custom Cursor Highlighting
✅ Built-in Screenshot Utility
✅ Lightweight & Optimized Engine (Built on Electron & FFmpeg)
--------------------
System Requirements:
--------------------
OS: Windows 10 / Windows 11 (64-bit)
Processor: Intel Core i3 or AMD equivalent (or higher)
RAM: 4 GB minimum (8 GB recommended for 60 FPS recording)
Storage: 200 MB free space for installation
